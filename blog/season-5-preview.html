Eggy Party Forest Fairy Tale Season Guide: Stages, Events & Dates

Updated September 25, 2026 · Based on the official September 23 update notice
← Back to Blog
Forest Fairy TaleSeason GuideUpdated September 25, 2026

Eggy Party's Forest Fairy Tale Party Season launched on September 25, 2026. This page replaces our former speculative Season 5 preview with confirmed details from the game's September 23 official update notice. The season's new Party Season goals run through November 19, 2026.

At a glance

Season start: September 25, 2026 at 00:00 · Season goals end: November 19, 2026 at 23:59 · New stages: 10 · Ranked: new season is live and Egg Rank resets under the Fright Fest inheritance rules.

All 10 new Party Season stages

  1. Beast Invasion
  2. Flip & Fold
  3. Storybook Journey
  4. Serpent Valley
  5. The Crocodile's Maw
  6. Diving Pool
  7. Woodland Encounter
  8. Forest Shadows
  9. Flipping Gale
  10. Forest Battle

The official notice confirms the stage names, but does not publish route maps or shortcut details. Treat any route advice as hands-on player tips rather than official map data.

Ranked reset: what to check

Ranked Parties for the new season are live. Egg Rank rewards have been updated, and rank inheritance follows the rules for Fright Fest. Check the in-game Ranked Parties screen or the official account for your exact starting rank and rewards; the announcement refers players there for the detailed inheritance table.

Eggy Island additions

The Forest Fairy Tale season also updates Eggy Island. The announcement highlights feeding chickens, tapping a hopping frog, avoiding the crocodile in the lake, and climbing mountains to ride a giant paper crane. A Giant Egg Yolk arrived on the island at 00:00 on September 25; approach and talk to it to discover its interaction. The new Building Giveaway Office lets players view the Eggy City construction site and operate a tower crane.

Confirmed event calendar

  • Forest Fairy Tale Party Season goals and Party Season Story: September 25–November 19. Weekly goals award tokens exchangeable for listed rewards; viewing the story also gives rewards.
  • Golden Autumn Holiday Top-Up: September 25–October 15.
  • Super Scratch-Off: warm-up tickets are available September 25–30; the official event opens October 1 and runs through October 11. The notice says up to seven tickets can be earned.
  • Party Season Origami Guide: September 25–November 19.

Event availability and rewards may vary by server. Check the in-game event page before spending currency or planning around a deadline.

Other September 23 update note

The update also launched a gyroscope feature for Battle Vanguard. It can be enabled and adjusted under the mode lobby's Features and Settings menus. This is a separate mode update, not a Forest Fairy Tale stage mechanic.

Source: Eggy Party official: September 23 Update and No-Downtime Maintenance. Dates and event details above follow that notice; check the game for later changes.
